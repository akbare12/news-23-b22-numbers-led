# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, led by the day's numbers.

**The day in numbers:** −3.3% Kospi · 5.3 US–China power gap · 11,762 Ebola cases (DRC) · 10T won Seoul rescue fund · 2% billionaire tax plan · $450M Skubal Mets deal · 165 Russian missiles in a week · 12,000 cleared in Manchester · 264 kg explosives seized in Lebanon · $107.3B Anthropic IPO.

**The three big stories:** the AI rout that hit Asian markets (Kospi −3.3%, closes 4,708.54), UN chief Guterres urging global AI safety rules at the Bridgetown summit, and the US lead over China falling to a record low (Asia Power Index: US 78.9, China 73.6, gap 5.3).

**31 stories, 6 regions**, each with a named source and a certainty chip (REPORTED / CLAIM / ONGOING), plus a world map with 31 numbered pins and a DATE CHECK box that lists what was left out because it happened on another day — and what is still developing.

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies the copy is 1024×1024 with a matching SHA-256. `draw.py` draws the image with Pillow.
